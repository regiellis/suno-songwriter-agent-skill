# Jazz, Stage, And Screen-Song Writing

Use for melodic forms, character-driven songs, and broad scene or industry labels that need a specific musical treatment. For instrumental scoring use `instrumental.md`; for timed screen edits use `screen-music.md`.

## Jazz Standards And Vocal Jazz

Determine whether the user wants a newly written standard-like song, an arrangement of their melody, a blues, a modal vocal piece, or a contemporary jazz-pop song. These are different assignments.

For a 32-bar AABA plan, four eight-bar sections form a common layout. The B section contrasts with A; A may contain the title or refrain within itself. It is not automatically a modern verse/chorus structure. Write phrase arrivals that make the return to A recognizable. ABAC and other forms are also available.

Write lyrics to the melodic sentence and harmonic arrivals. A line can begin before the bar or continue across it. Avoid making every harmonic change require another word. Leave space for held notes, pickups, and instrumental replies. Scat is optional and should not replace requested lyrics without reason.

When the requested performance includes improvisation, plan a melody statement, an instrumental space, and a return if useful. A prompt describing improvisation cannot certify its musical quality or exact length.

## Modal Jazz And Fusion

A vamp can develop through motif, register, rhythm, and interaction rather than frequent chord changes. For jazz fusion, identify what is being fused: improvisational phrasing, harmony, a rhythm-section approach, electronic texture, or another tradition. `Jazz chords` alone do not define the piece.

For a jazz/hip-hop blend, choose whether the fixed beat supports a repeating sample-like harmony or live interaction. Keep the rapper's rhyme landings audible. For a jazz/rock blend, decide where an instrumental lead has room to develop rather than soloing continuously over a dense vocal.

## Musical Theater And Character Songs

Establish who sings, whom they address, what they want now, and why speaking no longer suffices. A scene song can change tactics rather than simply intensify emotion: charm, bargain, accuse, admit, decide. Let the musical form follow those changes.

A recurring phrase can return with a new intention. A reprise may be brief because the audience already knows the material. A duet needs different objectives or information for its singers; alternating identical feelings does not create dramatic exchange.

Original scene plan:

```text
Character: a station clerk trying to persuade a traveler to stay.
First tactic: delay departure with practical questions.
Second tactic: offer reasons the destination is disappointing.
Turn: admit that the plea is personal.
Return: the original practical phrase now becomes an honest request.
```

Do not force a chorus or rhyme onto every line. Use patter for purposeful verbal momentum, not to cram an entire plot summary. A sustained phrase can reveal what the character could not say quickly.

## Chanson, Cabaret, And Art Song

For chanson or cabaret, specify the speaker's stance and the relationship with the listener: intimate account, satire, confession, performance persona. Accordion and cafe ambience are optional timbres, not a writing method. Let rhetoric, diction, and a recurring line or melodic idea organize the song.

For an art-song treatment, decide whether the text suggests strophic, varied-strophic, or through-composed development. The accompaniment can respond to, contradict, or anticipate the voice. Do not add a pop chorus to a through-composed request. Preserve supplied original text according to the requested edit scope.

## J-Pop, J-Rock, City Pop, And Screen Songs

Treat these as broad fields. Choose a specific rhythmic and harmonic direction, then fit the language to the melody. Japanese phrase design may depend on mora timing and natural word grouping; romanization length is not a syllable or bar count.

For a driving J-rock screen opening, a recurring riff can frame a verse, pressure-building section, and refrain. For a quieter or unconventional opening, use its actual brief rather than forcing speed and maximal energy. A 90-second cut is an arrangement assignment, not permission to rush every lyric.

For city-pop-oriented writing, let bass, chord color, and vocal phrasing interact; do not rely solely on a retro synth label. For an ending song, the needed emotional function can be closure, ambiguity, or contrast rather than automatic softness.

## K-Pop, Korean Ballad, And Korean Indie

K-pop can use sharply contrasting sections, multiple vocal roles, rap, or a post-chorus, but none is required in every song. Make a melodic or rhythmic element recur across the contrasts so they belong to one song. A rap section should serve a role, not appear merely because the genre label was given.

For a Korean ballad, budget breath, tessitura, and the approach to a sustained emotional phrase. Do not require a key change or constant belting. For indie, folk, hip-hop, or R&B, select the relevant writing family and use natural Korean diction. Language alone must not determine genre. English code-switching is an option only when requested or justified by the brief.

## South Asian Film/Pop And Arabic Pop

A film industry is not a single musical system. Identify the language, dramatic purpose, and contemporary or tradition-specific direction. A dance number, ghazal-influenced song, intimate ballad, and raga-based composition need different decisions. Do not add tabla and sitar to every South Asian brief.

For raga-based work, a scale label alone is insufficient: melodic behavior and rhythmic organization matter. Carnegie Hall's [Indian Classical teaching material](https://www.carnegiehall.org/Education/Programs/Musical-Explorers/New-York-City/Falu) introduces raga and tala as distinct foundations. Verify a requested raga's behavior and tala rather than inventing a chord progression and claiming equivalence.

For Arabic pop, specify the regional language/dialect, phrase design, and desired rhythmic or modal treatment. If a maqam is requested, do not equate it with a Western scale preset. [MaqamWorld](https://www.maqamworld.com/en/maqam.php) explains melodic pathways and constituent ajnas. Describe what is known, research precise details when needed, and treat generated tuning and ornamentation as listening checks.

These are routing safeguards and starting decisions, not complete instruction in those systems. For multilingual adaptation use `multilingual.md`; for a contemporary hybrid name the actual musical roles in `genre-fusion.md`.
